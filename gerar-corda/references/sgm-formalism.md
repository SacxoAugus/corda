# CORDA operational formalism (SGM)

## Principle

CORDA is a probe and a projection of a dynamic field. First look for the
structures the formalism requires; only then compile. The metaphor of
strings, branes, gravity, and event horizon helps show internal state,
interactions, authority, protection, and time. It does not prove that
organizations obey high-energy physics.

## Four axes

| Axis | Operational question | Observable examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| X — Operation | What moves or gets delivered? | task, flow, deadline, capacity |
| Y — Value/Environment | Why does value exist and who captures it? | revenue, cost, user, market |
| Z — Governance | Who decides, and under what constraints? | contract, policy, access, gate |
| W — Cognition | What internal state alters the decision? | intent, confidence, memory, bias |

W requires evidence or a hypothesis label. Do not infer psychic state as
fact.

## Primitives

### Bulk

Define boundary, available memory, and the observed period. Whatever is
outside enters as environment or external source.

### Brane or mode

Model a unit capable of holding state and acting: person, AI agent, team,
function, component, machine, discipline, or lens. Keep:

- identity, role, and contribution to X/Y/Z/W;
- cognitive mass and autonomy, when observable;
- active, new, shielded, or archived state;
- base model, evidence, context, prompt, and execution;
- shared, private, and tool-obtained evidence topology;
- source of the mapping.

### Closed string

Model internal processing with no external commitment yet. Keep the
question, evidence considered, confidence, emission condition, and last
check. Do not expose private chain of thought.

### Open string

Model an observable interaction: task, decision, dependency, message,
resource, or handoff. Keep origin, destination, content, tension, lead
time, state, and source.

### Synthesis desk

Integrate contributions and make convergences, conflicts, causality, and
gaps explicit. Weights must reflect evidence and independence, not the
number of modes or prestige. Correlated convergence is a weak signal, not
corroboration.

A second round exists only when an `evidence_delta` comes in. Repeating
the same evidence with a different mode, prompt, or model does not create
a new observation.

### Gate and observer

Test evidence, coherence, safety, authority, and exit condition. Record
whether the gate shares a model, evidence, or context with what it
audits. A correlated gate can find defects, but it is not an independent
observer.

### Shielding

Represent confidentiality, segregation, permission, compliance, security,
IP, or personal data. Name a rule and an owner; a blue halo with no rule
is not shielding.

### Entropy

Measure loss of currency or coupling due to age, inactivity, or absence
of an owner. Actions: `refresh`, `rebind`, `archive`, or `escalate`.

### Human boundary condition

Identify who accepts the decision. The system recommends, blocks, or
escalates; it does not create authority.

## Projection semantics

- Center: integrator or synthesis.
- Individual halo: closed W loop.
- Wavy line: interaction/open string.
- Brightness/thickness: tension or mass.
- Distance: autonomy, only with an explicit rule.
- Blue halo: shielding with an owner.
- Heat: entropy or critical deadline.
- Dark disk: archive.
- Lower ring: gate.

## Validity criteria

A valid CORDA:

1. passes the applicability gate;
2. defines source, boundary, and owner;
3. contains the four axes;
4. distinguishes internal state from external interaction;
5. preserves conflict and uncertainty;
6. maintains traceability;
7. lists what did not fit;
8. lists what the formalism required and did not find;
9. classifies independence and convergence;
10. blocks rounds with no new evidence;
11. separates compilation from evaluation and promotion;
12. separates neutral runtime from physical overlay;
13. does not mistake metaphor for a verified computational mechanism.

If the gate returns `NOT_APPLICABLE` or `INSUFFICIENT_INPUT`, do not
fabricate a runtime just to complete the design.
